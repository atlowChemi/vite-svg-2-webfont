Designs of the same icons to combine into one [family](/guide/features/variants), such as light and bold. Needs at least two designs with unique names and at least one SVG each, and exactly one default. Weights run from 1 to 1000 and increase in list order; designs without one get a weight automatically, following [these rules](/guide/features/variants#rules). Families support TTF, WOFF, and WOFF2 only.
